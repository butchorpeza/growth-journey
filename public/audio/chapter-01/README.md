# Narration recordings

Butch's own recordings go here, one MP3 per narration, named by its id. For example:
- `survival-lamp.mp3`
- `survival-computer.mp3`
- `shadows-portrait.mp3`
- `shadows-descent.mp3`

The words for each are the panel text in `src/data/chapter01.ts` and `src/data/chapter02.ts`, and the lines in `SHADOWS_LINES`.

Nothing is here yet, and nothing is faked: the voice is switched off (`NARRATION_ENABLED` in `src/audio/narrator.ts`), and Chapter 02's lines show as subtitles only. Switch the voice on once the recordings exist.
