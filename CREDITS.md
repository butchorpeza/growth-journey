# Credits

The Growth Journey uses the following third-party assets. CC0 assets need no credit; they are listed for transparency.
CC-BY assets are credited as their licence requires. The pause menu and the website footer link to this list.

## Character
- **MPFB2 / MakeHuman** (body, skin, eyes, face parts, clothing): MakeHuman Community, https://static.makehumancommunity.org. MPFB add-on GPL-3.0; generated characters and core and system assets CC0.

## Lighting
- HDRIs from **Poly Haven** (CC0): Overcast Soil (Pure Sky), Mud Road, Farmland Overcast. https://polyhaven.com

## Chapter 01 · Survival
- Models and materials from **Poly Haven** (CC0): modular electricity poles and cables, plastic monobloc chair, wooden table, plastic jerrycan, wooden bucket, enamel pot, barrel stove, wicker basket, sungka board, vintage oil lamp, wooden crate, old tyre, wooden ladder, rusted spade; rusty corrugated iron, red laterite soil, concrete block wall, weathered planks, damaged plaster, muddy tracks. https://polyhaven.com

## Animation
- Character motion capture from **Mixamo** (Adobe), https://www.mixamo.com: Breathing Idle, Male Standard Walk,
  Male Walk Backwards, Standard Running, Standing Left/Right Turn, Jumping Up, Mid-Air Falling Idle, Landing,
  Picking Up An Object, Idle Stand Looking Around. Retargeted onto the MPFB character and baked into its model;
  the raw Mixamo files are not distributed.
